Our Story

A Taste of Punjab,
Served with Love.

From rich Punjabi flavours to comforting North Indian favourites, discover a dining experience inspired by the warmth, generosity and spirit of Punjab.

Think slow-simmered gravies, breads pulled hot from the tandoor and the easy hospitality of a shared table — a place to gather, linger and come back to.
